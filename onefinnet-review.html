Three people, three different frustrations

Founders, highly ambitious: “We’re growing fast — we can’t afford slow hiring.” They want to build a dream team without losing speed, and look for a partner tool that brings structure to chaos.

Recruiters, no time to breathe: “Juggling 10 open roles, 5 tools, and 0 time.” They want a clearer view of pipelines, feedback and performance, and wish for a single dashboard that tracks everything in one place.

Candidates, straight to the point: “Excited for opportunities, disappointed by silence.” They hope for clarity, respect and real updates, and judge a company’s culture by how they are treated during hiring.

The Product

A single platform to automate hiring end to end

An AI hiring assistant that works as hard as you do. Automated shortlisting, engagement tracking and real-time insights keep every team member in sync and in control.

The goal was to give recruiters the same clarity AI already brings to every other business function — so hiring feels strategic rather than stressful.

Recruiters juggle endless candidate lists and follow-ups; the intent was to equip them with real-time insight and let them make faster, smarter, more confident decisions.

One surface for sourcing, screening and decisions, instead of a stack of tools that never agree.

Job Creation

Stages of the job creation flow

, body: [

This flow is not only about user experience, it is about conversion and trust. Recruiters already using another tool do not want to learn something new — they want to do the same work faster and smarter. So it had to fit existing habits: upload an old JD, save drafts, keep the settings customisable.

The selected iteration runs in four stages. Enter basic job details, where the form fills in the basics and suggests what else the role might need, saves progress as a draft, and reads an existing JD to understand context. Customise your screening criteria, where AI builds a snapshot of the ideal candidate and the recruiter reweights what matters most. The candidate application form, which starts from a ready-made base with the must-have screening fields. Then configure and publish — per-job settings, a final review before going live, and a privacy and terms acknowledgement.

Optimising it cut friction, automated the repetitive parts and delivered context-driven recommendations, shortening the turnaround on creating a job.

AI Reports

Leading with the data that decides

, body: [

The rejected flow failed in three specific ways. It dead-ended: once a recruiter opened a profile there was no clear next action except going back to the candidate list, which broke the workflow and killed momentum. The report was textual, so users had to read, interpret and only then decide — draining when repeated across dozens of candidates. And basic information sat below the fold, so getting to it required a scroll, adding task time and frustration.

Each report section was restructured to lead with the most relevant data, which meant categorising and prioritising it by how much it actually mattered to a recruiter. Every visual and content block was then designed to minimise cognitive load, so attention goes to evaluating talent rather than decoding a report.

Admin Panel

Laying the foundation of the admin panel

Laying the foundation of the admin panel

The goal was to give complete control back to the organisation’s core — the admins. From the control panel they invite recruiters, hiring managers and teammates, granting each the right access and visibility through Role-Based Access Control.

Every complex product needs a defined control structure that shares information on a need-to-know basis, and RBAC is what makes that enforceable rather than a convention.

It had to be a scalable system rather than a static interface — hundreds of recruiters and thousands of candidates, still fast and still reliable as the organisation grows.

Learnings

Finally, my top learnings

, body: [

Designing the data. Deep candidate and hiring analytics had to feel effortless and contextual, not heavy — the reference points were analytics tools that make complex data look lightweight.

The importance of trust. Hiring data is sensitive, so every number, insight and automation had to be trustworthy through transparency, feedback loops and a clean audit trail.

Using AI responsibly. The challenge was not to automate blindly but to assist intelligently, keeping humans in control while AI handled the heavy lifting.

Designing for scale and change. Teams, roles and data all grow, so the admin panel had to be a system that absorbs that rather than an interface that breaks under it.
